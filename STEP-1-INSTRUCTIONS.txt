STEP 1 — CLASS FILTERING

1. Open APPS-SCRIPT-CODE.gs and copy all code.
2. In Google Sheets: Extensions > Apps Script. Replace the old code and Save.
3. Select setup and click Run. It adds a classGrade column to Students.
4. Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil > New version > Deploy.
5. Existing students: open Students and type their class in the new classGrade column.
6. The student class must match the exam class. Capital letters and extra spaces do not matter.
7. Open index.html and test with two different classes.
