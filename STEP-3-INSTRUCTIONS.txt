STEP 3 — TEACHER APPROVAL

1. Replace the Google Apps Script code with APPS-SCRIPT-CODE.gs and Save.
2. Select setup and click Run. This adds approvalStatus to Teachers.
3. Existing teachers are automatically marked approved.
4. Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil > New version > Deploy.
5. A new teacher is saved as pending and cannot log in.
6. To approve: open Teachers and change pending to approved in approvalStatus.
7. To block: change it to rejected.
8. Use only approved, pending, or rejected.
